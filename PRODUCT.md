# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, and potential clients reviewing Chorn Chhery's professional background and work.

## Product Purpose

A local-first personal portfolio that presents verified work experience, projects, technical skills, education, and ways to view the resume or get in touch.

## Positioning

The portfolio shows practical software work evidenced across factory operations and production systems, AI/RAG applications, and full-stack development. This is a presentation of documented work, not a claim of industry ranking or comparative performance.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse an individual static web page, inspect local project screenshots, follow external repository/demo/profile links, download resume PDFs, view the education transcript, or open an email/contact link.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The site is a plain local HTML/CSS/JavaScript project in `D:\Resume`, as selected by the user. Keep the current stack and avoid unnecessary dependencies. Preserve working local PDFs and asset paths. Do not add a fake form or claim availability, certifications, achievements, or other facts not verified in the supplied resume/site.

## Evidence on Hand

The existing `index.html` and `project.txt` contain experience, projects, skills, education, profile and contact links; the workspace contains the portrait, project screenshots, `ChornChhery_Resume.pdf`, `ChornChhery_Resume_1page.pdf`, and `transcript.pdf`. These real materials are the source of truth. No certification data or real form-submission service is evidenced.

## Product Principles

- Make documented professional value easy to scan and verify.
- Prefer concrete work details and real project evidence over unsupported claims.
- Keep every contact, download, repository, and demo action honest and functional.
- Preserve a light, understandable local implementation that is easy to maintain.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, keyboard-operable navigation and interactions, visible focus, meaningful image alternatives, responsive reflow, and respect for reduced-motion preferences as specified in the user's brief.
